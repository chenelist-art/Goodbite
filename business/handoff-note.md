# GoodBite B2B app handoff note (7 Oct 2026)

Read with `claude/goodbite-handoff.md` (consumer prototype and videos) and `claude/goodbite-b2b-pivot.md` (why B2B).

## What was built
A new, separate demo: **GoodBite for Business**. A supply-chain portal plus till (POS) for small independent F&B outlets in the Klang Valley, with a practice version of LHDN e-invoicing. The consumer prototype was not changed.

- Live link: https://claude.ai/artifact/Y7qBYUDmxhh4ACBLmewGCM (private until Chen shares it from the Share menu)
- File: `Goodbite/business/index.html` on Chen's computer (single file, no backend, data saved in the browser).
- Source: `business/source/` (`src/` has style.css, core.js, data.js, ui.js, s_*.js, tour.js; `build.py` joins them). `python build.py` makes two outputs in `dist/`: `index.html` (full page, has Print and Download buttons) and `artifact.html` (for the Claude link, uses Copy boxes because the preview blocks printing and downloads).
- Tests: `business/source/test/audit.js` (clicks every button in every role) and `flow.js` (types through a full day and checks the numbers). They need Node and Playwright.

## What is in the app
- 3 roles with a visible switcher (no login): Owner (8 menu items), Cashier (Home, Sell), Kitchen (Home, Stock, Orders, Waste).
- Sell: 44 menu items with options, dine-in/takeaway, hold/resume, void with reason, 4 pretend payment types, service charge + SST + 5 sen rounding (all switchable in Settings), receipt, recipe-based stock deduction.
- Stock: 60 ingredients kept by batch with expiry, oldest used first, low and expiring lists, stock count, auto-waste when a batch expires.
- Orders: 6 suppliers, 3-step order, suggested reorder, receive goods (part deliveries), supplier bills with payment and e-invoice status.
- Waste: 3-tap log, monthly report (kg, RM, top items, reasons, food cost %), CSV as a copy box, Food Rescue with made-up partners.
- Invoices: 4-step e-invoice from a sale, combined (consolidated) e-invoice for walk-in sales, credit note, cancel within 72 hours, statuses Draft/Submitted/Valid/Invalid/Cancelled, fake ID and QR, JSON in a simplified UBL 2.1 layout, printable view, DEMO banner on every e-invoice screen.
- Guidance: 5-step welcome tour, 12-step Demo tour for the 5-minute presentation, Help on every page, "?" tips for technical words.

## Second round, same day (menu editing + languages)
- Settings > Menu and recipes is now a full editor: add dish, change dish (name, group or new group, price, picture, options, recipe rows), delete dish with confirm and Undo. New dishes get codes from 601. Till tabs come from the dishes, so a new group appears as a new tab.
- Languages: English, Bahasa Melayu, Simplified Chinese. Switch in Settings > Language or in the Help pop-up. Stored in `settings.lang`.
- How translation works: the app is still written in English. `src/i18n.js` watches the page and swaps each piece of text using `src/dict.js` (about 1,440 entries; exact phrases plus patterns like `{n} items expiring in {n2} days`). `dict.js` is generated by `test/mkdict.py` from `test/tr1..tr8.txt` and the harvested phrase lists. To add or fix a phrase, the simplest way is to add a line `English || Malay || Chinese` to `test/tr8.txt` and run `python3 test/mkdict.py` then `python3 build.py`.
- Not translated: user-typed names, demo company names, dates, CSV text, e-invoice JSON.
- The Malay and Chinese were written by Claude and not checked by a native speaker.
- Bug fixed along the way: typing in a box and then clicking a button straight away could swallow the click (the page redrew between press and release). Affected Tax settings too.

## E-invoice facts checked on 7 Oct 2026 (sources are inside the app under Invoices > E-invoice notes)
- Exemption: LHDN e-Invoice Guideline v4.8 (30 Aug 2026) says turnover under RM3,000,000 is exempt. LHDN's timeline web page still says RM1,000,000 (updated 7 Dec 2025). The app follows the PDF and flags the mismatch. **Either way most target customers are exempt**, so e-invoicing is a "nice to have / future-proof" selling point, not a legal driver. Worth raising with the supervisor.
- SST: food and drink is 6% service tax; registration only above RM1.5 million yearly sales (Customs MySST FAQ).
- Cancel window 72 hours from validation (MyInvois SDK). Consolidated e-invoice within 7 days after month end, buyer "General Public", TIN EI00000000010, classification 004 (Specific Guideline v4.9, 7 Sep 2026). Single sales above RM10,000 cannot be consolidated from 1 Jan 2026.
- Not confirmed: SST on service charge, how service charge appears on an e-invoice, 5 sen rounding source, MSIC code, TIN format, penalty grace period end date.

## Test results (final build, after the second round)
- Button audit in English: 13,408 clicks across three runs (started as Owner, Cashier, Kitchen) at desktop width, 0 failed. This includes the new menu editor and Language screens.
- Same audit with the app in Chinese (Owner run, 4,828 clicks) and Bahasa Melayu (Kitchen run, 4,346 clicks): 0 failed. These check buttons, not wording quality.
- Flow test: 34 of 34 checks passed. Menu editor test: 9 of 9 passed (add dish with new group and recipe, sell it and see stock drop, change, delete, undo).
- Translation coverage: every phrase the audit saw on screen (about 5,700 strings) has a Malay and Chinese version, apart from names, dates, IDs and addresses. Messages that only appear after unusual typing may still show in English.
- After the audits finished, two small changes were made and not re-audited: on phones the Demo tour button was removed from the top bar (still in Help), and one translated word was corrected.
- Screen sizes: main pages checked at 1366, 1024 and 390 px for sideways overflow and console errors (none). The click-everything audit ran at desktop width only. Not tried on a real phone.
